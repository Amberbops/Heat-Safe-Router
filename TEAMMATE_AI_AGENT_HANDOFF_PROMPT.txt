WHAT /plan-route ALREADY DOES
GET /plan-route?originLat=...&originLon=...&destLat=...&destLon=...

1. Validates coordinates.
2. Gets current weather from Open-Meteo.
3. Gets up to 3 walking alternatives from HeiGIT/openrouteservice.
4. Uses route geometry and OSM route metadata.
5. Segments routes.
6. Reads surface and waytype metadata.
7. Calculates environmental exposure proxies.
8. Calculates Relative Heat Exposure Score.
9. Ranks Fast / Balanced / Heat-Safe routes.
10. Generates request_id.
11. Persists a compact route-plan summary to DynamoDB.
12. Returns route geometry and analytics as JSON.

The frontend should call API Gateway, not HeiGIT/Open-Meteo directly.
